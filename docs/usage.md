# User guide

RetroBoard Lite has one page: title and timer at the top, three columns below, and three buttons under the
columns. The buttons move down as the columns grow. (The app itself is in Swiss German; button names are quoted
as they appear.)

| What | How |
|---|---|
| **Title** (optional) | Type it at the top, e.g. «Sprint 42». It appears in the copied result. |
| **Timer** | Pick «3 Min.», «5 Min.» or «10 Min.», then **▶ Start**. While running: **⏸ Pause**. **↺** resets. At 00:00 the display blinks and a gong sounds. **🔔 / 🔕** switches the gong on or off (switching on plays a short preview). |
| **Write a note** | Type into the field of a column and press **Enter**. The cursor stays in the field, so the next note follows straight away. |
| **Number** | Each note has a fixed number at the bottom right (#1, #2 …) — «let's look at #7». It never changes when sorting; a deleted number leaves a gap. |
| **Vote** | Click **👍**; the figure shows the votes. Notes stay where they are. **−** takes a vote back. The note with the most votes in a column gets a **red frame** (all notes on a tie). |
| **«Nach Stimmen sortieren»** | Sorts every column once, most votes first. The order then stays until you click again; new notes are added at the end. |
| **Show a note large** | Click the text of a note. **‹ Zurück** / **Weiter ›** or the arrow keys browse through all notes; **Schliessen**, Esc or a click beside the note closes. |
| **Delete a note** | Click **×** in the top right corner of the note. |
| **«Ergebnis kopieren»** | Copies all notes as text — sorted by votes, with number and «Top-Thema». Paste it into the Teams chat or an e-mail. |
| **«Neue Retro»** | Deletes all notes and the title (asks first). Copy the result before! |

## Good to know

- Everything is saved automatically in this browser. Another browser or laptop does not see the retro.
- The app loads nothing from the internet and sends nothing away; even the gong is generated in the browser.
- The gong needs a click on **▶ Start**, because browsers only allow sound after a user action.
- Please do not enter customer or personal data.
- Works by double-clicking `index.html`, on GitHub Pages, in light and dark mode, and on a phone.
